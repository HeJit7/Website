Psychology Program at International Black Sea University

International Black Sea University · Tbilisi, Georgia

Psychology at International Black Sea University is a bachelor's program in Tbilisi, Georgia, taught in English. Duration: 3 years. Tuition is about $4,900 per year (fees checked October 2026; confirm with the university).

Duration: 3 yearsLanguage: EnglishDegree: Bachelor'sTuition: $4,900 / year

About this program

The English-taught psychology program introduces major areas of psychology, research methods, applied theory, communication and professional ethics. A bachelor’s degree is generally a foundation for further study rather than an automatic qualification for independent clinical practice.

Admission requirements

Secondary-school completion or equivalent and the international admissions procedure; verify current English evidence and required documents.

Application deadline & intake

Confirm current intake and deadlines with IBSU international admissions.

Career opportunities

Potential fields include HR and recruitment support, research assistance, community and education projects, user research, training coordination and support roles in relevant organizations. Clinical or counselling practice generally requires further education, supervised practice and local licensing.

Career pathways are illustrative. They are not a promise of employment, professional recognition or licensure; regulated professions may require additional exams, registration or supervised practice.
