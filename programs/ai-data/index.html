How much does it cost to study AI & Data in Georgia?

Among the English-taught AI & Data programs in our database, annual tuition for international students ranges from about $1,150 to $5,500, depending on the university and program. Confirm exact fees with the university.

Which universities in Georgia offer AI & Data in English?

5 of the 14 universities in our database: Alte University, Caucasus University, The University of Georgia (UG), Georgian National University SEU, Kutaisi International University.
