This Privacy Policy explains how StraightToGeorgia collects and uses personal information when you visit our website, submit a contact form, ask for personal guidance or communicate with us about studying in Georgia.

1. Who we are

StraightToGeorgia is an educational guidance service based in Tbilisi, Georgia. For the purposes of applicable data protection law, the organisation or person operating StraightToGeorgia is responsible for deciding how and why personal information is processed (the “Data Controller”).

2. Information we collect

When you use the website contact form, we may collect the information requested by that form:

  • your name and country of residence;
  • your preferred field of study or academic programme;
  • your preferred contact method (for example, WhatsApp or email) and the contact number or email address you provide;
  • the university you are asking about, if any;
  • your preferred language;
  • your enquiry or message;
  • your privacy acknowledgement or consent record, and the date and time of your request.

The current contact form is not intended to collect passports, academic certificates, transcripts or other application documents. Please do not submit such documents through the initial contact form. If you later ask us to assist with an application, we may request additional information or documents that are reasonably necessary for that specific assistance. We will explain why the information is needed and how it will be used when requesting it.

3. Why we use your information

We use the information submitted through the website to respond to your enquiry, contact you using your chosen method, understand your study interests, and provide the educational guidance you request. This may include explaining university options, programme details, admission requirements, application steps and relevant practical information about studying in Georgia. If you later request application support, we may also use relevant information to help organise application documents or communicate with a university, but only where this is necessary for the assistance you have requested and permitted by applicable law.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process contact-form information on the basis identified in the form and applicable law, including your consent where consent is requested. Where you ask us to take steps before entering into an arrangement for services, we may also rely on the legal basis applicable to responding to that request. Where another legal basis is relied upon, we will apply it only where its legal requirements are met.

You may withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before the withdrawal.

5. When we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only where reasonably necessary and lawful, including:

  • with a university or educational institution, where you ask us to assist with an application and sharing the relevant information is necessary for that request;
  • with service providers that support our website or operations, such as website hosting, email, form handling, storage or spreadsheet services;
  • with public authorities or other parties where disclosure is required by law or a valid legal process.

We do not send your contact-form details to a university merely because you submitted an initial enquiry. We do not authorise a service provider to use your information for its own unrelated marketing.

6. Service providers and international transfers

Some technology providers may process information on servers located outside Georgia or outside your country of residence. Where applicable law requires safeguards for an international transfer, we will put the required safeguards in place. Our current service providers are Cloudflare (website hosting) and Google (Gmail, Google Sheets and Google Apps Script, used to receive and store contact-form enquiries). You can ask us for more information about them at any time.

7. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or for longer where required by law. 8. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you and the circumstances of the processing, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction or blocking of processing, or information about how your information is used and shared. You may also have the right to object to certain processing, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority. To make a privacy request, contact us at straighttogeorgia@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

9. Cookies and analytics

This website does not use tracking cookies. Essential technical functions, security logs or similar technologies may still be used by the website infrastructure where necessary to operate and protect the site. If analytics, advertising pixels or other non-essential tracking technologies are introduced, we will update this Policy and obtain consent where required by law.

10. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. Access should be limited to people who need the information to respond to your request or provide the service. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Third-party websites

The website may link to university websites, government portals, social media platforms and other third-party websites. Those websites operate independently and have their own privacy practices. Please review their privacy notices before providing information to them. We are not responsible for privacy practices on websites we do not control.

12. Children and applicants under 18

Our website is intended for people interested in higher education. If an applicant is under 18, additional safeguards or involvement of a parent or legal guardian may be required depending on the applicant's age, the service requested and applicable law. Please contact us before submitting information about a minor.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes to our services, website, technology or legal obligations. The current version will be published on this page and the “Last updated” date will be revised.
