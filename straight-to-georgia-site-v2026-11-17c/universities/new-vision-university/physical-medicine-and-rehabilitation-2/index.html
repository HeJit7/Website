Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation Program at New Vision University

New Vision University · Tbilisi, Georgia

Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation at New Vision University is a master's program in Tbilisi, Georgia, taught in English. Duration: 2 years. Tuition is about $4,500 per year (fees checked October 2026; confirm with the university).

Duration: 2 yearsLanguage: EnglishDegree: Master'sTuition: $4,500 / year

About this program

The four-year bachelor's programme introduces physical medicine and rehabilitation, human function, recovery and multidisciplinary support for people affected by injury, illness or disability. The specific professional scope depends on the awarded qualification and the rules of the country of employment.

Admission requirements

International applicants should provide a passport, school-leaving certificate, academic records and application. Confirm English-language evidence, document recognition and programme-specific admission requirements with NVU.

Application deadline & intake

Confirm the next intake and deadline with NVU.

Career opportunities

Rehabilitation centres, hospitals, multidisciplinary clinics, community rehabilitation services and further study. Regulated clinical roles may require additional qualifications or registration.

Career pathways are illustrative. They are not a promise of employment, professional recognition or licensure; regulated professions may require additional exams, registration or supervised practice.
