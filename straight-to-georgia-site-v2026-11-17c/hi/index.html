Frequently asked questions

Whatever you need on the way to studying in Georgia, ask us — we help you find the answer and the next step.

What can you help me with?

Choosing a university and program, checking admission requirements, preparing and reviewing your documents, the application, the invitation letter, visa or residence-permit paperwork, arrival and finding a place to live. If something is unclear at any step, ask us and we work out the answer with you.

Is your guidance really free?

Yes. Our guidance for international students is free. University tuition, application fees, translations, visa fees and other third-party costs are paid separately, directly to the university or the office concerned.

Do I need a visa?

It depends on your nationality. We check the rules for your country and help you prepare the study-visa or residence-permit documents.

Which documents do I need to apply?

Usually your passport, school certificate or diploma with transcripts, proof of English, and photos. Some programs (for example medicine) ask for more. We give you a checklist for your chosen program and check your documents before you submit them.

Do I need IELTS or TOEFL?

Not always. Many universities accept other proof of English or run their own English test or interview. We tell you what your chosen university accepts.

When should I apply, and what are the intakes?

Most programs start in autumn (September–October) and some also have a spring intake. Deadlines differ by university, so apply as early as you can. We tell you the current deadline for your program.

Is my degree recognized abroad?

Many programs follow ECTS, and medical programs often follow WFME standards. Recognition depends on the country where you want to work, so we look at your case and point you to the official requirements.

How much does it cost to study in Georgia?

Tuition for English-taught programs is listed on every university and program page. Living costs in Tbilisi are roughly $400–$1,000 per month, including accommodation, food and transport.

Where will I live?

Some universities have dormitories, others do not. Private apartments are common. We help you look at options and understand the contract before you pay.

Can my parents talk to you too?

Yes. Parents are welcome on calls and WhatsApp. We explain each step in simple words so the whole family knows what comes next.

Can you guarantee admission or a visa?

No one can honestly guarantee that: the university decides on admission and the authorities decide on visas and residence permits. What we do is help you choose realistic options and prepare a complete, correct application.

What if I run into a problem after I arrive?

Write to us on WhatsApp. Whether it is paperwork, housing or a question about your university, we help you find the right next step.
