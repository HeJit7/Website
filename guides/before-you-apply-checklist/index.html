What to confirm with a Georgian university before you apply (2027 checklist)

Fees, intakes and requirements can change. The directory we use to compare universities (checked 2 October 2026) recommends confirming the points below with the university before you pay or submit anything.

  1. Confirm that the program you want is currently accepting international students.
  2. Confirm the exact intake (start date) and the application deadline.
  3. Get the final tuition invoice in writing, with the currency and what it includes.
  4. Ask whether there is an application or registration fee, and whether it is refundable.
  5. Check the English-language requirement (certificate or interview).
  6. Ask how your previous education documents are recognized and what the process is.
  7. If you apply through an agency, check the university's own rules for agency applications.

Things we noticed at specific universities

  • Alte University: $250 non-refundable application fee.
  • Caucasus University: Grenoble MBA (€18,900) is a full-program price.
  • European University: Business program fees are not listed separately — confirm with the university.
  • The University of Georgia (UG): Contact: International Student Enrollment Manager.
  • Georgian National University SEU: Master's business fee ($7,450) is the published annual fee — confirm full price and scholarship terms. Data Science and AI master's fees are not published.
  • International Black Sea University: Scholarship up to $500 on Medicine is not guaranteed.
  • Georgian American University: GAU & IPAG Double Degree MBA (€21,000) is a full-program price.
  • East European University: Listed annual fee includes health insurance.
  • New Vision University: Public contact is the call center.
  • Ilia State University: Two intakes per year — confirm the exact deadline.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to pay before I receive an invoice?

You should wait for the university's written offer and final invoice. Confirm payment details directly with the university.

Where do the contacts in this guide come from?

From the public admissions contacts listed in the Georgia International Admissions Directory (checked 2 October 2026). Contacts can change, so confirm them on the university's official website.
